Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Introduction

ManyLattice ("we", "our", "us") operates DebtPayoff, a debt payoff planner for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the app.

DebtPayoff is designed to work on your device. Your financial details - the names of your debts, balances, interest rates, minimum payments, payment dates, the payments you record and your monthly budget - stay on your device and, only if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud account. We cannot read them.

DebtPayoff does not connect to your bank, does not ask for bank or card credentials and does not offer loans or credit products.

Two things leave your device: anonymous usage analytics and crash reports. Neither contains any amount, interest rate or debt name. Both are described in full below.

Information We Collect

Financial Details You Enter

  • DebtPayoff stores the debts you add (name, type, balance, interest rate, minimum payment, payment day), the payments and balance updates you record, planned one-time extra payments, your monthly budget, your payoff strategy and your currency.
  • This information is saved in the app's local database on your device. Your payoff plan is calculated on your device.
  • iCloud sync is off by default. It is a PRO feature that you can turn on in Settings. When it is on, your data syncs through Apple's CloudKit service to your own private iCloud database, so it is available on your other devices. This copy belongs to your Apple Account. We do not operate a server, we have no account with access to it, and we cannot view your data.
  • We never transmit your financial details to us or to any third party.

Exports

  • With PRO you can export your payoff schedule as a CSV or PDF file. The file is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it through the iOS share sheet.

Usage Analytics

DebtPayoff uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used. Firebase is provided by Google LLC and acts as our data processor.

  • We collect anonymous usage events that describe how the app is used - for example that the app was opened for the first time, that a debt was added, that the plan screen was viewed, that the payoff strategy was changed, that a payment was marked as paid, or that the subscription screen was shown.
  • These events carry only categories, never your financial details. They never include amounts, balances, interest rates, budgets, debt names, notes, your name or your contact details. Where an event needs context, it uses a fixed category such as the debt type ("credit card"), the number of debts as a range ("2-3"), or the chosen strategy ("snowball").
  • Alongside these events, Firebase processes technical data: a Firebase app instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, language and region, and your IP address. Firebase derives a coarse, city-level location from the IP address. We have turned off collection of the advertising identifier and the device vendor identifier, and we do not receive or use precise GPS location.
  • The app instance identifier is not linked to your name, your Apple Account or your financial details. It identifies an app installation, not a person, and is reset if you delete and reinstall the app.
  • We use this only to understand which parts of the app people find useful and where they get stuck.
  • We do not use analytics data for advertising, we do not enable Google Signals or ad personalisation, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Crash Reports

DebtPayoff uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix crashes.

  • If the app crashes, a crash report is sent the next time you open it. It contains technical information about the crash: the state of the app's code when it crashed, device model, operating system version, app version, free memory and disk space, and a Crashlytics installation identifier.
  • Crash reports do not contain your financial details, debt names or any other content you entered.

Google's handling of analytics and crash data is governed by Firebase's privacy documentation and the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

  • If you turn on payment reminders and allow notifications, DebtPayoff schedules local reminders on your device before your payment dates.
  • These reminders are generated and delivered entirely by your device. No notification content is sent through our servers or any third-party push service.
  • You can turn reminders off in the app or withdraw notification permission at any time in iOS Settings. The app continues to work without them.

Purchases

  • DebtPayoff offers optional PRO subscriptions and a one-time lifetime unlock. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store.
  • We receive from Apple only the information needed to confirm that your PRO access is active. We never receive your payment card details, billing address or Apple Account credentials.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not require an account, a login, an email address or a phone number. DebtPayoff works without you telling us who you are.
  • We do not connect to your bank, card issuer or lender, and we never ask for banking credentials. Every figure in the app is one you entered yourself.
  • We do not upload your debts, payments, balances, budget or exports to any server we control.
  • We do not use advertising, ad networks or ad tracking. The app contains no advertising SDK, does not collect the advertising identifier and does not ask for permission to track you across other apps and websites.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone, and we do not share it with lenders, credit bureaus or debt relief companies.
  • We do not collect your precise or GPS location, and the app never asks for location permission. Our analytics provider derives only a coarse, city-level location from your IP address, as described above.
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, calendar, health data, microphone input or camera input. The app does not request access to any of these.
  • We do not send your data to any external AI or cloud processing service. All payoff calculations run on your device.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share information only in these limited cases:

  • Apple - for optional iCloud sync of your own data to your own private database, for App Store purchase processing, and for delivering local notifications on your device. Apple's handling of this data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Google LLC (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) - for the anonymous usage events, crash reports and technical data described above, and only those. Google acts as our processor and is not permitted to use this data for its own advertising purposes.
  • Legal requirements - if required by law, we may disclose information we hold. In practice we hold no financial details, so there is very little to disclose.

Legal Basis for Processing (EEA and UK users)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we process data on the following bases:

  • Performance of a contract - processing your purchase and entitlement information so we can provide the PRO features you paid for.
  • Legitimate interests - usage analytics and crash reports via Firebase, used to understand, fix and improve the app. We limit this data so it contains no financial details and no directly identifying information such as your name or contact details, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Consent - notifications, which you grant explicitly through an iOS permission prompt and can withdraw at any time in iOS Settings.

Your Rights

Because your financial details live on your device and, if you choose, in your own iCloud account, you already have direct control over them:

  • Access and export - your data is visible in the app at any time, and PRO users can export their schedule as CSV or PDF. Data stored in your private iCloud database is also covered by Apple's own data access and export tools for your Apple Account.
  • Correction - you can edit your debts, payments and budget directly in the app.
  • Deletion - you can delete individual debts and payments in the app. Deleting the app removes its local database from your device. To remove the synced copy, delete DebtPayoff's data from iCloud in iOS Settings under your Apple Account.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or another region with comparable law, you also have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data we hold about you, to request restriction of or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. This includes the right to object to our use of analytics and crash reporting based on legitimate interests.

Please note the practical limits on what we can look up: we never hold your financial details, and analytics and crash data are tied to an installation identifier rather than to your identity. To act on a request about this data, we would need you to supply that identifier, which is not visible in the app. In most cases the fastest and most complete remedy is to delete the app, which stops all further collection from that installation.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in the Contact section below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

Your California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion or correction of it, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

The categories of personal information we collect are:

  • Identifiers - a Firebase app instance identifier and a Crashlytics installation identifier, plus your IP address. Business purpose: analytics, crash diagnosis and product improvement.
  • Internet or other electronic network activity - the anonymous in-app usage events and crash reports described above. Business purpose: analytics, crash diagnosis and product improvement.
  • Geolocation data - a coarse, city-level location derived from your IP address by our analytics provider. We do not collect precise location. Business purpose: understanding which regions the app is used in.
  • Commercial information - whether you hold an active PRO entitlement, as reported by Apple. Business purpose: providing the features you paid for.

The financial details you enter stay on your device and in your own iCloud account; we do not collect them. We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined by the CPRA, and we do not use or disclose personal information for purposes beyond those listed above. We retain each category only as described in the Data Retention section.

International Data Transfers

Firebase analytics and crash data are processed by Google LLC on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where data is transferred out of the EEA or the UK, we rely on the safeguards Google provides for these transfers, including the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission and Google's participation in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Your financial details are not transferred by us at all - iCloud sync, if you turn it on, happens between your device and your own Apple account, governed by Apple's terms.

Data Retention

  • Your financial details - kept on your device until you delete them or delete the app. The iCloud copy, if sync is on, is kept in your Apple Account until you remove it.
  • Analytics events - retained by Firebase for up to 14 months, after which event-level data is deleted automatically. Aggregate reports that cannot be traced back to an app installation may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports - retained by Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Purchase and entitlement records - held by Apple according to Apple's own retention policies.

Data Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security, including the device passcode and iOS file encryption, and by Apple's security for iCloud data. We use standard measures to protect the limited data we handle. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

DebtPayoff is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time - for example if we add a feature that handles data differently. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Significant changes will be reflected in the app.
